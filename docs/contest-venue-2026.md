# Contest venue reconstruction

## Verified information

- YTV's 2026 overview specifies a launch platform 10 m above water, a 10 m runway, a 3.5 degree slope and a fan-shaped deck. Public viewing capacity is approximately 300 seats, not a guaranteed count for every stand.
- Shibaura Institute of Technology's September 3, 2026 report contains actual July 25 contest photographs: a dense galvanized scaffold tower, long low pier, inclined access ramp, shore-side white tents, pine trees, spectator structures and an LED screen truck.
- The university reports approximately 70 university/parent supporters attending. This does not establish their exact seating location or a reserved 70-seat allocation.

## Inferred geometry

The simulation is a photo-informed reconstruction, not a survey or a certified digital twin. No publicly available construction drawing establishes the deck width, fan curvature, scaffold spacing, ramp length, pier elevation or seating coordinates used here.

- Launch center is Z=0; shore is modeled at Z=-160 m. Pier width is 3 m, low deck elevation 1.1 m, ramp extends from Z=-60 to -18 m. These are approximate scene dimensions.
- Rear staging deck width is 14 m; curved launch deck maximum width is 18 m. Rear staging is level and joins the sloping 10 m runway continuously.
- 300 chairs in ten stepped rows represent the published approximate total public capacity. Seventy blue-shirt supporters within this seating model and the TBT banner are illustrative, not an asserted reserved seating plan.
- Tent count, headquarters location, cameras, screen graphics, surrounding houses, shore shape and tree distribution are illustrative. Only S-320 remains the selectable aircraft.
- Generic event signage is newly drawn; no official logo or reference photograph is copied into the website. Screen truck shows static event/team information, not a live broadcast.
- Existing map coordinates/turning points remain approximate. The shoreline and wave foam now use the same shared shore coordinate; launch physics remain unchanged.

## Viewing

Flight camera choices: launch platform, full venue, supporters' stand, chase, pilot and wing. Starting a flight from a stationary venue camera switches to chase. Railings are placed on the approach, not across the launch path.
